## Explicit Distance Regimes

The next engineering review examined the [paper's model boundary](https://thales.mit.edu/bush/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/BazantBush-PNAS2021.pdf),
pp. 9-10. Its continuous-jet correction is unmasked. Spatially uniform risk
does not itself define a positive minimum separation; using it as the complete
airborne-distance criterion additionally neglects local enhancement. Reduced
mask penetration alone does not establish that approximation. This review was
not supplied to the research ledger as evidence or as a case assumption.

The existing conditional-limits tool now offers two routes for the same requested
`risk_threshold_distance_m`, without adding a report quantity or changing cases:

- The original continuous-unmasked-jet kernel, with its existing constraints.
- An explicitly selected `negligible_near_field` model condition. Its
  steady-background criterion returns no positive minimum under R_in <= epsilon,
  or no finite separation meeting the criterion above that boundary. Variable
  duration produces a numerically bound relationship, not an invented duration.

The new route is not automatically selected for masks. It supplies no disease
value, candidate hypothesis, evidence record or support judgment. The
investigation must propose and assess the physical premise in context; a weak
guess can be calculated, but receives no evidential endorsement. Mask leakage,
thermal plumes and other spatially varying exposure remain outside this route.
It is not a general masked-jet model, nor permission to assume away local risk
to finish the report. The report preserves the selected regime and interprets
zero as no positive minimum in that model, never as a contact-safety claim.

Dependency routes, root and child model context, calculation provenance and
duration-record recognition now retain this distinction. The new duration
record carries version 2 plus its regime; existing version-1 jet and R_in
relationships retain their interpretation. Contradictory distance conditions
cannot form one compatible parameterization. No near-field numeric inputs are
required or credited as dependencies of the spatially uniform route.

Source review also found that `output_is_resolved` allowed an inapplicable
kernel to count toward output credit and report completion. Domain exclusions
now remain available for dependency propagation and parent feedback but do not
resolve a requested numerical output. The three required output families are
unchanged; excluding a model is not a substitute for calculating the quantity.

These changes have been reviewed only by reading source. No tests, import or
compile checks, replay, or new live provider call were run. The V16 artifacts
remain unchanged and do not exercise this route. Its numerical implementation,
agent selection and evidence justification need live verification after Argo
access is legitimately restored. The goal is unmet; required live verification
is blocked by the provider's explicit monthly access revocation. This same
condition has persisted through the V16 outcome, control/communication edits,
and this distance-regime follow-up. No further provider requests or substitute
provider were used to circumvent it.
